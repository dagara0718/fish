## research ↔ SpeciesProfile

Every non-`undefined` field in `species-profiles.ts` traces to a cited claim in
`research/species-environment-evidence.md`. Fields the research doc marks "근거 부족 — v1.4 미사용"
are `undefined` on the profile (감성돔/농어: no `preferredWaterTemperature`, no
`seasonalActiveMonths`; no species anywhere has a current-speed/tide/time-of-day field, because none
was added to the `SpeciesProfile` type at all — there was nothing to cite).

## official index ↔ environment guidance separation

- Distinct `assessmentType` (`OFFICIAL_FISHING_INDEX` vs `ENVIRONMENT_BASED_GUIDANCE`), distinct
  React components (`species-grid`/`species-card` vs `guidance-grid`/`guidance-card`), distinct CSS
  namespace, distinct section heading and kicker text.
- `computeEnvironmentGuidance`'s only inputs are `OfficialItem[]` (raw KHOA fields), the point, and
  `today` — it never receives `species: OfficialSpeciesIndex[]` or any `officialGrade`/`officialScore`
  value. Confirmed by the function signature and by the identical-output test in both
  `environment-guidance-provider.test.ts` and `live-provider.test.ts`.

## trust propagation

`EnvironmentBasedSpeciesGuidance.trustStatus` is independently computed (`UNVERIFIED` or `STALE` from
the same freshness rule as the official index) and independently propagated through the
`STALE_CACHE` fallback path. It is never derived from `suitability` — a `HIGH` result with
`UNVERIFIED` trust is directly exercised by every non-stale test fixture.

## unsupported species / unknown factor semantics

- "기타어종" has no `SpeciesProfile` entry and never will without a cited species-level claim — it is
  structurally impossible for it to appear in guidance output, not merely filtered at render time.
- `UNKNOWN` is a first-class `FactorResultStatus`, never a 0/default numeric score, and is excluded
  from the `evaluable` count in `computeSuitability` rather than counted as a mismatch — confirmed by
  the "never a fabricated match" tests in `species-guidance-scoring.test.ts`.

## Known, documented limitation (not a defect)

농어 and 감성돔 can only ever reach `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` in this release, because this research
pass found no cited season/current/tide/time claim for either. This is called out explicitly in
`v1.4-product-delta.md` "Suitability rule" as the correct, honest consequence of thin evidence — not
something to work around by relaxing the minimum-evidence rule.
